What you are looking at

A tiny BERT: 8 tokens in, a few of them hidden as [MASK], and the model learns to guess the hidden ones. It is so small (6 numbers per token) that you can see every single number it computes. Nothing here is useful NLP — the point is to build intuition for what attention does.

Colours
weights & activations: blue = negative, orange = positive, faint = near 0
probabilities (softmax outputs): darker green = closer to 1
one colour per attention head, used everywhere
Try this (2 minutes)
  1. Press → a few times (or click the stage buttons) to walk through one forward pass.
  2. Hover any number in a computed matrix: the exact arithmetic appears and its inputs light up.
  3. In the bottom panel press Step ×100 a few times, or Train. Watch the loss fall and the attention heatmaps sharpen.
  4. Click a token box in the top bar to mask a different position and see how the prediction changes.
  5. Choose the task Your data… to train on your own sequences — ready-made example sets are provided.
